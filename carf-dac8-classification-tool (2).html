Crypto-Asset Reporting Classifier

A self-assessment tool for CARF (OECD) and DAC8 (EU) crypto-asset tax reporting obligations — built for use by any exchange, broker, wallet provider, or crypto platform.

Not legal or tax advice. This tool is an educational decision-support aid built from the OECD's published CARF Model Rules, Commentary and FAQs, and the EU's DAC8 Directive (2023/2226), plus any jurisdiction notes added in the Library tab. It does not cover every domestic implementing law, many of which are still being finalised. Confirm any classification with qualified tax/legal counsel and your competent authority before relying on it.

Built from the OECD CARF Model Rules, Commentary & FAQs (December 2025 edition) and EU Directive (EU) 2023/2226 (DAC8). Last reviewed 11 August 2026. This page runs entirely in your browser — no data is sent anywhere. Free to copy, adapt and self-host.
